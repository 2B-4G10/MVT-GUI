# MVT GUI Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 28, 2026*

MVT GUI checks phones for known signs of spyware. It was built so that
nothing about you or your phone ever has to leave your computer.

## What we collect

Nothing. MVT GUI has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash
reporting of its own. We do not receive, store or sell any information
about you, your computer or the phones you check.

## What stays on your computer

The backups, bug reports and other files you choose to check are read on
your computer only. The results are written to a folder you choose. You
decide whether to share them with anyone, for example a digital security
expert.

## When the app goes online

- **Indicators of compromise.** When you download or update indicators, the
  app fetches public indicator files from GitHub and the sources listed in
  MVT's indicator index. These are plain downloads: no information about
  you or your phone is sent with them.
- **VirusTotal (optional, off by default).** If you turn on the VirusTotal
  lookup and enter your own VirusTotal API key, MVT sends the hashes of
  files found on the phone to VirusTotal to look them up. This only happens
  when you ask for it, and is governed by VirusTotal's own privacy policy.

Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with developers when
you allow it in your Mac's settings. That is controlled by Apple and by
you, not by the app.

## Children

MVT GUI is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page
with a new date.
